# 0050: Front panel PCB and display modes

Status: Open
Opened: 2026-11-27 (owner request)

Front panel of the MEGA-KVM case (0048):
* 16x2 HD44780 LCD with an I2C backpack (or 40x2 if similar in price);
* per DUT a red LED (powered) and a green LED (selected), for 8 DUTs;
* a few momentary buttons: input selection (next/previous) and mode
  (scan / fixed / PIP);
* master on/off rocker with backlight (wired to the power board, 0047).

A small PCB on the master shield's I2C header: an I2C expander (e.g.
TCA6424A or 2 x PCA9555/TCA9535) for 16 LEDs and the buttons, with
interrupt output; the LCD's backpack on the same bus. JLCPCB-assembled,
with LCSC parts.

Display modes (FPGA/host features, to design when the panel exists):
* **fixed:** today's behaviour (one selected DUT);
* **scan:** cycle through the DUTs that have video, with a settable dwell;
* **PIP:** selected DUT full screen plus small live inset(s) of others,
  reusing the thumbnail store (0035/0036).

## 2026-11-27 lead

The shield's front-panel header is 1x6: GND, 5V, 3V3, SDA, SCL, INT (I2C
pulled up to 3.3 V). The front-panel board carries a PCA9306 to the LCD
header, and a jumper chooses a 5 V or 3.3 V LCD supply (owner: 5 V
backpack or a cheap 3.3 V LCD, either fine).

## 2026-11-27 front-panel board v0.1 (agent, branch feature/front-panel)

KiCad 10 project `hardware/mega-kvm-front/`, generated by Python
(`tools/build_all.sh`, same approach as the power board). Details, bit
map and panel coordinates: `docs/hardware/front-panel.md`; machine-readable
panel features for the case generator: `hardware/mega-kvm-front/out/panel-holes.json`.

* Board 227 x 36 mm, 2 layers, vertical, 3 mm behind the panel with the
  component side facing the panel. Left to right: 1602 LCD, 8 LED columns,
  5 buttons. The master rocker sits in the panel to the right of the board
  (off-board, cabled to the power input board J5).
* LCD module mounted on the **back** of the board (M2.5, 75 x 31 mm); its
  bezel passes through a 73 x 26 mm board window to the panel. A PCA9306
  feeds the backpack header J2 (GND/VCC/SDA/SCL). JP1 (1x3 + shunt) picks
  a 5 V or 3.3 V VLCD.
* 3 mm THT LEDs (red C84774, yellow-green C85161) flush on the board; the
  lenses sit inside 3.2 mm panel holes, so no light pipes are needed.
  6x6 THT switches with a 9.5 mm stem (C136679): PREV, NEXT, MODE, SPARE1,
  SPARE2.
* Two TCA9535PWR (TSSOP, no exposed pad): **0x25** LEDs (P0 red/powered,
  P1 green/selected, active low), **0x26** buttons (P0.0-4, active low,
  /INT to J1.6; P1 on a DNP 1x10 EXP header). All U2 pins have 10 k
  pull-ups. The LCD backpack stays at 0x27/0x3F.
* Every BOM line has an LCSC number (JLC search, 2026-11-27). Passives are
  basic parts; the ICs, LEDs, switches and headers are extended.
* ERC 0. DRC 0 violations, 0 schematic-parity issues; **unrouted** (132
  unconnected pads). `tools/route.py` does a Freerouting DSN/SES round trip
  but needs the owner to run Freerouting, or the board to be routed by hand.
* Open: route the board; measure the LCD module (bezel height for the
  spacer, viewing-area position); confirm the 3 mm panel gap and button
  height against the case design (0048).
