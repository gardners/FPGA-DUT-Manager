MEGA-KVM link-board shield for the Numato Mimas A7 V3 (issue 0045). One shield per Mimas, two Mimas per unit.

* Plugs onto Mimas P12 and P13 with 2x40 female sockets on the shield's bottom side.
  Rows 34-39 (FPGA configuration pins) are not connected on the shield.
  Rows 1-2 (VIN) are fed only from the 12 V input (Mini-Fit Jr, power board 0047 always-on output).
* Rear edge, shield level: JB1 2x6 JTAG/UART for DUT0-3 in the overhangs beside the Mimas
  (shortest JTAG routes), HDMI inputs for DUT1-3 above the Mimas. DUT0 video = Mimas HDMI IN.
* Rear edge, Mimas level: the DUT keyboards (J5 2x5) directly below their JB1, on two keyboard
  sub-boards (separate KiCad project kbd-sub/) joined by 2x6 board-to-board headers.
* Front edge (RJ45 side): physical keyboard (J5 incl. JTAG; short ribbon to the rear panel),
  I2C header to the front-panel board (0050) with 5 V from a 12->5 V buck, keyed power-control
  header (master only), 12 V in, neighbour UART (3-pin).
* I2C: TCA6424A @0x22 (revision, features, PWR_CTL0-7), TCA6416A @0x20 (sense, HPD, JTAGEN, PRESENT).
* Header pin plan and expander maps: docs/hardware/shield-pinplan.md v1.
