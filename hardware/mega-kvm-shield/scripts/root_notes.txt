MEGA-KVM link-board shield for the Numato Mimas A7 V3 (issue 0045). One shield per Mimas, two Mimas per unit.

* Plugs onto Mimas P12 and P13 with 2x40 female sockets on the shield's bottom side.
  Rows 34-39 (FPGA configuration pins) are not connected on the shield.
  Rows 1-2 (VIN) are fed only from the 12 V input (power board 0047, always-on output)
  and passed to the neighbour shield through a polyfuse.
* Rear edge (Mimas HDMI side): DUT1-3 HDMI inputs, DUT0-3 JTAG+UART (MEGA65 JB1 pinout)
  and keyboard (MEGA65 J5 pinout, KIO8/9/10 + 3.3 V sense only). DUT0 video = Mimas HDMI IN.
* Front edge (RJ45 side): physical keyboard (J5 pinout incl. keyboard JTAG), I2C header
  to the front-panel board (0050), power-control header to the power board, 12 V in.
* Side edges: neighbour link (UART + 12 V pass-through), 1x4 right angle, plus 1x3 Dupont header.
* I2C: TCA6424A @0x22 (P0 revision straps, P1 features, P2 power enables),
  TCA9535 @0x20 (DUT VIO/keyboard sense, HPD enables, HDMI +5V sense).
* The header pin plan (which FPGA pin carries which signal) is a PLACEHOLDER until
  docs/hardware/shield-pinplan.md (issue 0049) fixes it: see scripts/design.py HEADER_PLAN.
